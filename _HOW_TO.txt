WHAT CHANGED
The line was there because the nav is a solid white bar and the tint
stopped dead at it. The nav is now translucent with a blur behind it, so
the tint carries through the bar instead of ending at it. Same trick iOS
uses for its tab bar.

Also feathered the left and right edges of the tinted column, so on a wide
screen it fades out instead of ending on a ruled vertical line.

Hard-refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) after deploy.

IF YOU'D RATHER IT WERE OPAQUE
In TabShell.tsx the nav style now has:
  background: "rgba(255,255,255,.72)"
Raise .72 toward .9 for a more solid bar, or back to "#fff" and drop the
two backdropFilter lines to undo it entirely.
