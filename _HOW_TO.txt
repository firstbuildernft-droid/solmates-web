WHAT IT DOES
- Press: the button shrinks and dips slightly, shadow tightens, then
  springs back on release.
- Click: colour floods out from the centre to fill the circle, and the
  icon flips to white against it. Fades back after ~0.35s.

ONE CHOICE I MADE
Each button fills with ITS OWN colour: heart and star green, X red.
Filling the reject button green looked wrong next to a red X. If you
want all three green, say so and it's a one-line change.
