Scrollbars are hidden everywhere, in the settings panel and the rest of
the app. Scrolling works exactly as before -- wheel, trackpad, touch,
keyboard. Only the bar is gone.

Side benefit: the earlier tab-tilt fix relied on always reserving space
for a scrollbar. With no scrollbar to appear or disappear, there's
nothing left that can shift the layout.
