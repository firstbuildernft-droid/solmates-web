HOW IT WORKS
- Discover tab -> slider icon (top right) -> filter sheet slides up.
- Age: min/max boxes.
- Maximum distance: 10 / 25 / 50 / 100 / 250 km / Anywhere.
- Minimum wallet balance: Any / 1 / 5 / 10 / 50 / 100+ SOL.
- Verified only: wallet-verified OR face-verified.
- "Done" saves and instantly refetches the deck. "Reset" clears to defaults.

Filters are saved on your profile, so they persist across sessions and
devices -- not just this visit.

TWO DELIBERATE CHOICES (tell me if you want them changed):
- People with NO location still show up even with a distance filter set.
  Otherwise new signups would be invisible until they grant location.
- Setting a minimum SOL above "Any" implies wallet-verified, since we
  can't know an unverified wallet's balance.
