WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE
A soft green wash pooling at the top-left and bottom-right, fading to
white through the middle, with a fine grain over it. Cards, the nav bar
and the slide-in panels stay solid white, so they read as surfaces
sitting on the tint rather than everything going green.

The grain is a generated SVG, not an image file, so there's nothing extra
to download and it stays sharp on any screen.

TUNING IT YOURSELF
All of it is one block at the bottom of app/globals.css, in body::before.

  Too green?      lower 0.16 / 0.12 / 0.07 in the gradients
  Not green enough? raise them (0.24 / 0.18 / 0.11 is clearly visible)
  Too grainy?     lower opacity='0.16' in the SVG
  Grain too fine? lower baseFrequency='0.8' to about 0.5
  Hate it?        delete the whole body::before block; the four component
                  files can stay, they'll just show white again

Tell me which direction and I'll adjust it.
