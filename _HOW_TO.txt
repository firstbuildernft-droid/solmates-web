WHY IT WAS INVISIBLE
The backdrop was on body::before with z-index -1, which puts it behind
body's own background -- and body was set to solid white. So it was
rendering the whole time, just underneath an opaque sheet. It's now on
html itself, which paints across the full canvas and can't be covered.

I also nudged the green up slightly (0.18 / 0.14 / 0.08) since the first
pass was pitched conservatively and you never got to see it.

Hard-refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R after it deploys -- CSS caches.

TUNING

  too green      -> lower 0.18 / 0.14 / 0.08
  not enough     -> raise toward 0.26 / 0.20 / 0.12
  too grainy     -> lower opacity='0.16'
  grain too fine -> baseFrequency 0.8 -> 0.5
