WHAT CHANGED
The tint is now a fixed 430px-wide strip down the middle -- the same width
your screens use -- instead of covering the whole page. On a desktop
browser the app reads as a phone sitting on a plain white surface. On an
actual phone it fills the screen, so nothing looks different there.
