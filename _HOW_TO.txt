WHAT IT DOES
- Press: the whole tab shrinks and dips a touch, then springs back.
- Tap: a green pill grows out from behind the icon, then fades.
- Unread badges sit above the pill so they stay readable.

The pill is a soft green (22% opacity) so the icon stays visible through
it. Want it stronger, or the icon flipping white like the swipe buttons?
Say so.
